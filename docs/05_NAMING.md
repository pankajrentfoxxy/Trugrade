## How the numerology was computed

**Chaldean system.** Letter values:

| Value | Letters |
|---|---|
| 1 | A · I · J · Q · Y |
| 2 | B · K · R |
| 3 | C · G · L · S |
| 4 | D · M · T |
| 5 | E · H · N · X |
| 6 | U · V · W |
| 7 | O · Z |
| 8 | F · P |

There is no 9 in the Chaldean letter table — 9 is treated as sacred and only appears as a reduced total. The compound total is summed, then reduced by adding digits until a single digit remains (Chaldean does not preserve master numbers in this reduction).

**Your constraint:** the reduced total must be **5, 6 or 9**.

- **5** — communication, commerce, movement, adaptability. Suits a trading/marketplace name.
- **6** — trust, service, responsibility, care. Suits a warranty/QC-led promise.
- **9** — completion, mastery, wide reach. Suits a category-defining brand.

| **gorefurbo** | G3+O7+R2+E5+F8+U6+R2+B2+O7 = **42** | **6** | ✅ Passes. `gorefurbo.com` is already registered (presumably by you). |
| **rentfoxxy** | **42** | **6** | ✅ Passes. Already yours. |

## The shortlist

Ranked by how well the name carries the actual proposition — *we open every laptop, test it, grade it and seal it before you ever see it*.

### 1. NIKASHA — `nikasha.in` FREE
**Chaldean:** N5+I1+K2+A1+S3+H5+A1 = **18 → 9**
**Meaning:** From *nikash / nikasha* (निकष) — the **touchstone**, the black stone used for centuries to assay the purity of gold. A merchant rubs the metal on it and reads the streak. That is precisely what your QC executable does to a laptop.
**Why it wins:** the strongest name on this list. It is a real Sanskrit word, it means "the thing you test authenticity against", it is pronounceable to every Indian buyer and to a foreign investor, and it is not taken. Reduces to 9 — completion and mastery.
**Domains:** `.com` taken · **`.in` FREE** · **`.co.in` FREE`**
**Watch:** check for existing "Nikasha" fashion-label trademarks in other classes; Class 35/42 is likely clear but verify.

### 2. TASDIQ — `tasdiq.in` FREE
**Chaldean:** T4+A1+S3+D4+I1+Q1 = **14 → 5**
**Meaning:** Urdu/Hindi *tasdīq* (तस्दीक़) — **verification, attestation, certification**. In everyday north-Indian commercial speech it is the word for "getting something authenticated".
**Why it works:** six letters, two syllables, hard consonants, unmistakable meaning in your core market. Reduces to 5 — commerce and movement.
**Domains:** `.com` taken · **`.in` FREE** · **`.co.in` FREE**

**Why it works:** instantly comprehensible, and it says the thing your whole model rests on. Slightly long at eight letters. Reduces to 5.

**If you want meaning: Nikasha.** It is the only name here where the etymology *is* the business model — the touchstone that tells you whether the gold is real. `nikasha.in` and `nikasha.co.in` are both free.

**If you want the `.com`: Renuveon.** Abstract, clean, all three TLDs free, no trademark surface. You pay for it in advertising rather than in domain fees.

**If you want the shortest, sharpest thing: Yaqin.** Five letters, Chaldean 9 with no reduction, and it sells certainty rather than a process.

**And the honest default: keep gorefurbo.** It is already at Chaldean 6, you already own the domain, there is a design system built around the tri-arc logo, and the HTML prototypes already carry the wordmark. Renaming costs you a month of asset rework and buys you nothing you do not already have. The build prompt in this pack treats the brand as a **single configurable token** (`BRAND_NAME`, `BRAND_DOMAIN`, `LEGAL_ENTITY`), so you can defer this decision until Phase 5 without blocking anything.

## Before you buy anything

1. **Registrar check** — confirm at a registrar (not DNS). NXDOMAIN is a strong signal, not proof.
2. **Trademark** — search tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in in **Class 35** (retail/wholesale trading services) and **Class 42** (technology/testing services). Also check Class 9 if you will brand hardware.
3. **Company name** — check MCA name availability (RUN) if you intend to rename the legal entity. You do not have to: TrueTech Services Pvt. Ltd. can trade under a brand name.
4. **Buy defensively** — the chosen name across `.com`, `.in`, `.co.in`, plus the obvious typo variants, plus the matching handles on LinkedIn, X and Instagram, before you announce anything.
5. **DPIIT / startup registration** and GST trade-name update if the brand changes.
