The vocabulary

Three grades. That is the entire language.

Sellers cannot write their own condition description. They choose a grade, and our technician confirms or corrects it before the listing goes live. Cosmetic grade is never mixed with battery, warranty or working condition — those are always shown separately.

A+

Near-new

No meaningful marks at arm's length. No dents, hinges tight, display clean. There is genuinely nothing to disclose, which is why catalog photographs are enough.

A

Excellent

Minor hairline scratches or very light signs of use. No major dents, no display spots. What a well-kept two-year-old company laptop looks like.

B

Good, with visible use

Scratches and scuffs you will see; minor dents allowed. Nothing cracked, nothing missing. Four real photographs are mandatory, including a close-up of the worst defect.

Anything worse than B is not sold here. If a machine inspects below grade B it goes back to the seller. It never enters your order, and there is no override for it anywhere in our system.
